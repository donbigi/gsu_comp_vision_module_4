Why this works without any learning

The differentiation property of the Fourier transform says that taking a derivative is multiplying the spectrum by a ramp:

ℱ{∂f/∂x} = j·2πu · F(u,v)

so a gradient (an edge detector) is a high-pass filter in the frequency domain. The person is located two classical ways — skin colour (fixed YCrCb thresholds) for RGB, hot-body thresholding (Otsu) for thermal — and outlined with a spectral-residual saliency map, which is also computed entirely in the Fourier domain. See theory.md for the derivations and compare_sam2.py for the SAM2 comparison.
